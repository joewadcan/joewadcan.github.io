## Short Bio

I'm currently CEO and founder at Bevy Inc., makers of [Eventbot](https://geteventbot.com/). I also teach at UC Berkeley, focusing on [topics of programming and sales](https://joe.wadcan.com/projects/). Previously, I ran Business Development at GitHub and before that have started a few other companies. 

I also [invest in early stage startups](https://angel.co/joe-wadcan) and really enjoy mentoring underrepresented founders. If you'd like to chat, just send me [a note](https://joe.wadcan.com/contact) and we can grab a taco in the Mission district! 🌮 😃
