Sales is a learning function

1. Sales is not scaleable, why shouldn't I just make the product sell itself?

2. 



I just hired VP of Sales

Classic mistake. For first time CEOs, they see sales as this dark art and prefer to just hire away the problem. 

Founder has to make decision on over committing - seeing value in new ideas
